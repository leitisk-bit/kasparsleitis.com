How do I get my TCX file?

Garmin watches: start a Run activity, not Indoor Run, Treadmill or any other run. Only Run records the altitude that shows which floor you were on.

Garmin Connect (website, on a computer)
  1. Sign in at connect.garmin.com and open the activity.
  2. Click the gear icon ⚙ at the top right of the activity.
  3. Choose Export to TCX. The file is saved to your Downloads.
Garmin Connect app (phone)

The app cannot export files. Open connect.garmin.com in your phone's browser instead (choose “desktop site” if needed) and follow the steps above.

Strava

Open your activity on strava.com and add /export_tcx to the end of its address, for example strava.com/activities/123456789/export_tcx. Works only for your own activities.

Suunto

Should work if you can get a TCX file (for example through Strava), but it has not been tested yet.
