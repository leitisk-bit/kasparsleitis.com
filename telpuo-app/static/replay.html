Example map and runs. Open your map (PDF or image) and add your TCX file to replace it.
Draw: start at the start triangle and click along the way you ran. Set “Click adds” to Control when you click a control, or tap a placed point to switch it. When you change floor, pick Stairs and click the same stairway on the other floor. The last point is the finish.
Align: move the time to a moment you know (a control punch, the start, a stairway), then click where the runner really was. 1 point moves the track, 2 also rotate and scale it, 3+ average out GPS error.
