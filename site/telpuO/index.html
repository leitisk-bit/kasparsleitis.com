How the routes are found

The course map is read from its PDF: floors, stairwell letters, one-way stairs (red flights go up, blue go down), tape and one-way corridors. Every possible route between controls is listed, then matched against the time of each leg and the altitude the watch recorded every second. Floor changes show up clearly in the altitude, so the replay follows the stairs I really took, including detours.
